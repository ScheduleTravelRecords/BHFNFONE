BH FNF ONE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Summary

All user data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

BH FNF ONE is a local utility for executive schedules, travel segments, meetings, flights, and related itinerary notes. The app helps you organize agenda entries, archive history, and optional exports you choose to share.

Information We Collect

BH FNF ONE keeps the information you enter on your device. The developer does not operate remote servers to receive your schedule content, attachments, or preferences, and does not use in-app analytics or advertising tracking for this product.

Any text, dates, trip details, attachments, or display preferences you add are created by you and remain on the device unless you export or share them yourself.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • Agenda and archive records are saved in the app’s private storage area on your iPhone or iPad.
  • File attachments you add are kept in app-managed local folders, not uploaded automatically.
  • Settings such as display preferences are stored locally on the device.

Deleting the app removes app data from the device subject to iOS data removal behavior. Use in-app export features if you want a copy before removal.

Sharing and Export

When you tap share, export, or print actions, iOS shares only what you explicitly choose (for example JSON archive export, trip brief text, or PDF). Those flows use Apple’s standard share sheet; we do not receive a copy of shared content.

Import and Export

When you import or export files, you use Apple’s document picker or share sheet. Only the app or location you choose receives the file; the developer does not receive a copy.

Children’s Privacy

BH FNF ONE is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect product or legal changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when updates are published. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
